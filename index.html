Am I Eligible To Donate Today?

  • Age between 18 and 65 years
  • Weight above 45 kg
  • Hemoglobin level ≥ 12.5 g/dL
  • Last donation was at least 90 days ago (for Whole Blood)
  • No active fever, infection, or ongoing antibiotic treatment
  • No major surgery in past 6 months
